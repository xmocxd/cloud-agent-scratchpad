Constellations are cultural overlays on stellar geography. Orion, Ursa Major, and Crux are not physical families of stars; they are memory devices. Still, they remain the fastest way to learn the night sky and to point toward deeper objects such as a nearby nebula or a distant galaxy.

When you learn a constellation well, other chapters become easier to place. A wiki of the heavens works the same way: each named pattern becomes a door.
