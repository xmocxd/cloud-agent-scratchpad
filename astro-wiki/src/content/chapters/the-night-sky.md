Every culture has looked up and named what it saw. This book treats the night sky as both atlas and archive: a place where **constellations**, planets, and deeper questions about **galaxies** meet.

Before telescopes, sailors and shepherds tracked rising stars to mark seasons. Today those same patterns still orient beginners. Start with what you can see unaided, then let curiosity pull you toward darker, stranger topics like **dark matter** and the life cycle of a **nebula**.

## What this chapter covers

- How to find bright landmarks
- Why names matter for a living wiki of the sky
- Where to go next when a word sparks a question
