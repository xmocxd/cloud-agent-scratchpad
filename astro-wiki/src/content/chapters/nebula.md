A nebula is an interstellar cloud of gas and dust. Some glow from newborn stars; others are the shed outer layers of dying suns. Through a small telescope, a nebula can look like smoke caught in moonlight.

Nebulae sit among the **constellations**, but they are not patterns drawn by people — they are weather in the fabric of the **galaxy** itself. Studying them bridges mythic naming and physical process.
