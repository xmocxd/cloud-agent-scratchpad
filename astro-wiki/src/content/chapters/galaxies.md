Galaxies are vast systems of stars bound by gravity. Our own Milky Way is one among countless others. Through deep photographs, a galaxy can reveal spiral arms, dusty lanes, and bright cores.

Inside galaxies you will find star-forming **nebula** regions and the gravitational fingerprints of **dark matter**. The night sky is only the nearest edge of that larger ocean.
