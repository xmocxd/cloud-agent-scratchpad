Dark matter does not shine, yet it outweighs ordinary matter in the cosmos. Astronomers infer it from motion: stars at the edges of galaxies move too quickly to be held by visible mass alone.

You will not see dark matter in the night sky with your eyes. You will meet it as an explanation that ties **galaxies**, clusters, and cosmic structure into one unfinished story — a reminder that a wiki of knowledge always has pages still being written.
